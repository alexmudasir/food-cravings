# Food Cravings — Restaurant Online Ordering Website

Online ordering website for Food Cravings, a restaurant in Mirpur Khas, Pakistan. Built as a demo for the restaurant.

## What it does

- Full menu of around 130 items across 24 categories, with real prices in PKR
- Pizza step builder with per-size prices and add-ons exactly as printed on the menu
- Quantity steppers, search and sticky category pills
- Persistent cart bar and checkout that sends the whole order as one pre-filled WhatsApp message to the restaurant
- Admin panel (demo PIN 1234) to add, edit, remove and mark items sold out — changes are saved in the browser only

## Live demo

https://foodcravingss.netlify.app/

## Notes

- Food photos are illustrative AI-generated images; real photos will replace them once the restaurant provides them.
- No opening hours, delivery fee or delivery time are shown — the restaurant has not confirmed them.
- This is a demo. No online payment is included; orders go to the restaurant by WhatsApp.
